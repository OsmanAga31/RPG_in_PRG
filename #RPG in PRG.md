#RPG in PRG

##Implemented Features
- Chest's have Animation and Sound when Opening
- When switching the Scene there is a door Sound
- There are different items
- There is a item description when hovering over an item
- The inventory is persistent and the Status of chest's are too
- Scrolling in the inventory is more Pleasant now

##Visual Changes
- Inventory has a more appealing appearance now
